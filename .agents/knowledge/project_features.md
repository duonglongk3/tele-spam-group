# Aged Gmail Sourcing Bot Features & Specifications

This document outlines the requirements and system goals for sourcing vintage/aged Gmail accounts (2000-2025) for bulk procurement.

---

## 1. Bulk Procurement Scale & Pricing
* **Target Pricing**: Maximum target price is $0.5 per account.
* **Procurement Volume**: Unlimited daily purchasing capacity. The buyer continuously takes all available qualified wholesale stock from long-term, stable suppliers.
* **Payment Ready**: Fast checkout via USDT or Binance Pay upon verification.

---

## 2. Technical Quality Requirements (Verification Standards)
To prevent security locks and login failures, all sourced Gmail accounts must comply with:
* **Creation Age**: Vintage accounts created between 2000 and 2025.
* **No Hidden Phones**: The login process must not ask to verify with a phone number associated with the account profile.
* **No Instant Checkpoints**: Accounts must not lock, block, or demand captcha/verify immediately after login.
* **Untouched History**: No changes to any security information (passwords, 2FA, recovery emails) within the last 7 days.

---

## 3. Interaction Strategy
* **Group Sourcing**: Scans Telegram channels and groups to identify bulk email sellers and offer inquiries.
* **Private Negotiation**: Invites sellers to DMs to request test batches (1-2 accounts), negotiate prices, and establish stable contracts.
